DMARC setup for jorgeasaur.us

Current state:
- No TXT record exists at _dmarc.jorgeasaur.us.
- DNS appears to be hosted by GoDaddy / DomainControl.

Add this DNS record:

Type: TXT
Name / Host: _dmarc
Value: v=DMARC1; p=none
TTL: default

Optional reporting version:

v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@jorgeasaur.us; adkim=s; aspf=s

Use the optional version only if dmarc@jorgeasaur.us exists or points to a DMARC reporting service.

Notes:
- Only one DMARC TXT record should exist for _dmarc.jorgeasaur.us.
- p=none is a safe starter policy. It monitors mail without quarantining or rejecting messages.
- After SPF and DKIM are stable, the policy can move to quarantine or reject.

Verify after saving DNS:

dig +short TXT _dmarc.jorgeasaur.us

Then re-check the domain in Resend. DNS propagation can take a few minutes.
